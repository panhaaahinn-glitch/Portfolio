About

I'm a third-year Data Science student at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC). I'm passionate about data analysis, machine learning, and programming — and even more passionate about pointing all three at real problems worth solving.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of statistics and software: building the tools that clean and structure data, then the models and visuals that make sense of it. I like problems that start messy and end with a clear answer.

In the near term, I'm looking for an internship or entry-level opportunity where I can contribute to real data projects, learn from experienced teams, and keep building toward a career in data-driven decision-making.
